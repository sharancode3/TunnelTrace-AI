# RFC 9395
## Deprecation of 3DES and DES in IPsec

**Authority:** Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF)  
**Publication Date:** June 2023  
**Status:** APPROVED / ACTIVE / BEST CURRENT PRACTICE (BCP 238)  
**Document Code:** RFC 9395  
**URL:** https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9395  

---

### Section 1 Deprecation Rationale and Mandate
RFC 9395 formally deprecates the use of the Data Encryption Standard (DES) and Triple-DES (3DES / Triple-DEA) in the Internet Key Exchange Protocol (IKEv1 and IKEv2) and Encapsulating Security Payload (ESP):
- Both DES and 3DES utilize a 64-bit block size. Birthday attacks (including Sweet32, CVE-2016-2183) prove that after encrypting approximately $2^{32}$ 64-bit blocks ($32\text{ GB}$) under the same key, ciphertext collisions become highly probable, allowing practical plaintext recovery attacks.
- Implementations MUST NOT negotiate DES or 3DES in new IKE or ESP connections.
- Existing configurations still utilizing 3DES or DES MUST be migrated immediately to AES-GCM or AES-CBC with key sizes of 128 or 256 bits.
